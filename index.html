Benchmark Test Flow

This flow guides you through finding your new Working Max (WM) for either 5s or 3s hangs.

Suggested Warmup Protocol:

  • 5 mins progressive mobility & finger flexing
  • 1x5s Bodyweight hang on 20mm edge
  • 1x5s @ +10kg hang (light)
  • 1x3s @ +20kg hang (moderate)
  • Rest 3-4 mins before your first test single

Perform a maximal single hang. Log the load and the exact RPE felt.

🎯 **Goal**: Find the absolute maximum weight you can hang for the duration at a clean **RPE 9 to 9.5** (1 rep left in reserve, no grip breakdown).

⚠️ Guard Rail Note: This is a >15% jump from your previous WM. Was this a clean @9–9.5 with a touch in reserve? If it felt like a true ceiling (@10), consider entering a value 1–2 kg lower to protect your tendons.
